## Brand & Style

This design system defines a calm, organized, and inviting digital campus environment. Rooted in contemporary higher-education workflows, the visual language departs from legacy, clunky academic software in favor of crisp typography, spacious card-based groupings, and soft tonal hierarchy. 

The aesthetic marries **Corporate / Modern** precision with soft **Neo-SaaS** tactile accents:
- **Tone:** Academic, encouraging, dependable, and frictionless.
- **Target Audience:** University students, academic advisors, lecturers, and institutional staff handling heavy daily routines (course registrations, grade checks, attendance monitoring, and administrative clearances).
- **Core Principles:**
  - *Clarity Over Density:* White space and structured framing replace cluttered information matrices.
  - *Contextual Empathy:* Warm motivational banners and clear alert states guide students smoothly without bureaucratic stress.
  - *Semantic Predictability:* Critical actions (e.g., KRS submission, payment status) adhere strictly to established status color models.
